01 / QUALIFIED TECHNICAL & OPERATIONAL PERSONNEL

Skilled Labour Supply

GLCL coordinates certified machinery operators and technical supervisors for environments where precision, technical competence, and equipment safety are critical.
Modern container terminals and logistics yards operate under tight vessel schedules where equipment downtime translates directly into financial loss.
Every skilled candidate on our roster is verified through technical licensing, prior port experience, and reference reviews.
We ensure our clients receive operators who can step into high-tempo environments with full confidence and immediate operational capability.

  • Reach Stacker Operators: Container yard stacking, rail transfer, and vessel loading maneuvers.
  • Forklift Operators: Palletized cargo handling, container stuffing/unstuffing, and warehouse storage.
  • Crane Operators: Quay gantry, mobile harbor, and overhead crane handling.
  • Supervisors & Foremen: Berth coordination, shift scheduling, and gang safety management.
  • Tally & Documentation Clerks: Cargo manifest checking, waybill logging, and gate auditing.
  • Heavy Equipment Drivers: Internal terminal haulage and designated transit route transport.
Certification Verification: All skilled operator requisitions are matched against candidates holding accredited machinery licenses and validated operating hours.

02 / DISCIPLINED GENERAL WORKFORCE

Non-Skilled & Stevedoring Gangs

For general cargo handling, vessel hold operations, warehouse sorting, and physical labour requirements, GLCL provides disciplined workforce teams.
Stevedoring demands high stamina, coordinated teamwork, and strict adherence to maritime hatch protocols to prevent cargo damage and personnel injury.
Our non-skilled labourers work under designated lead men and allocators, ensuring full accountability throughout the shift.
Whether handling bulk commodities, bag sorting, container unstuffing, or wharf cleanup, our teams are mobilized on time and prepared for intense physical duty.

  • General Labourers: Warehouse stacking, pallet sorting, packaging, and facility upkeep.
  • Ashore Labourers: Wharf handling, hook unlatching, apron cargo sorting, and buffer transfers.
  • Transit Labourers: Corridor cargo transfer, cross-dock logistics, and inland container yard support.
  • Topside Labourers: Vessel hold rigging, cargo hook attachment, hatch squaring, and lashing duty.
  • Dedicated Shift Gangs: Assembled teams configured to client shift durations (day, night, and weekend berthing).
Worker Safety Protocol: Every gang member deployed through GLCL is required to wear standard personal protective equipment (steel-toe boots, helmets, high-vis vests).

OUR COMMITMENT TO CLIENTS

Reliable 24–48 Hour Response Framework

Get Labour Company Limited aims to review and respond to all manpower requisitions within 24–48 hours.
