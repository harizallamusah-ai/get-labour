WHO WE ARE

Over a decade of trusted manpower on Ghana’s waterfront.

Get Labour Company Limited was founded in July 2013 on Meridian Road, Tema, to bridge the critical gap between expanding maritime commerce and dependable, vetted frontline workers.
Under experienced leadership with decades of direct port operations experience, GLCL has grown into a cornerstone labour partner for leading terminals, shipping agencies, and manufacturing depots across Greater Accra and beyond.

OUR PROCESS

How labour coordination works with GLCL.

A transparent, responsive approach designed to keep port terminals, warehouses, and job sites moving without friction.

STEP 01

Request Submission

Submit your operational parameters online or via direct phone consultation. Specify gang headcount, shifts, certification requirements, and site location.

STEP 02

Roster Review & Selection

GLCL operations managers assess technical parameters and allocate verified candidates from our active roster matching your exact equipment and site standards.

STEP 03

Rapid Deployment & Supervision

Teams are briefed, equipped with appropriate PPE, and mobilized to your terminal or facility under dedicated gang supervision.
